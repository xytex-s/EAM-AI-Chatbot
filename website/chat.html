How it works

  1. You ask a question.
  2. The assistant searches a saved copy of about 140 public shu.ac.uk pages for the parts that best match it.
  3. A small AI language model, running on the project's own servers, writes a short answer using only those parts. The answer links to the pages it came from.
  4. Questions about food, coffee or shops near campus use OpenStreetMap instead.
  5. If a message suggests someone may be at risk, the AI is skipped. You see a fixed message written by people, with the university's own support links and phone numbers.

Privacy

  • Please don't enter personal details, such as your name, student number, address or details about your health.
  • We don't store your chat history. The conversation is kept only in this browser tab, and is gone when you close it or press "New chat".
  • Your questions are answered on the project's own servers. They aren't sent to an outside AI company.
  • For questions about places nearby, only the campus location and the type of place (for example "café") are sent to OpenStreetMap, never your question.
  • The only thing this page remembers on your device is whether you chose light or dark mode.
  • This is a student proof of concept built for an assessed university module. It is not an official Sheffield Hallam University service.
  • Answers can be wrong. Always check the linked page before acting on an answer.
  • For urgent help, call 999 in an emergency, or Campus Security on 0114 225 2000 (24 hours). You can also see the university's urgent help page (opens in a new tab).
